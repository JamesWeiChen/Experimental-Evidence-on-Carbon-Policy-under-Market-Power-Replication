# A7. Unused and constraining permits (firm-round)
# Data: subj_lv_panel.csv, Carbon Trading rounds (150 firms x 12 rounds), competitive benchmarks at eq_price from
# theory_benchmark_utils.compute_subject_theory_benchmark (realised cost draws). Standard errors clustered by session (10 clusters).
# unused = final_permits - emission (permits left over at the end of the round; final_permits = post-trading holdings);
# constrained = 1 if final_permits < competitive holdings at eq_price; short_of_efficient = max(0, competitive holdings - final_permits).
# Rows with negative unused permits: 0.
# Hypotheses: dominant - small under grandfathering; dominant - small under equal allocation; difference of the two;
# equal - grandfathering for small firms; equal - grandfathering for dominant firms.
# Standard errors clustered by session_code; stars use the wild-cluster-bootstrap p-value: * p<.05, ** p<.01, *** p<.001.
# p (cluster, normal): statsmodels cluster-robust p with a normal reference, the convention of the existing tables.
# p (cluster, t G-1): same statistic with a t(G-1) reference.
# p (wild cluster boot): restricted wild cluster bootstrap (Cameron-Gelbach-Miller 2008), Webb six-point weights unless stated.
